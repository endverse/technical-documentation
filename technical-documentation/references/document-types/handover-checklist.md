# Handover Checklist

Use when ownership, assets, materials, or long-term responsibility move to another party; do not use for a routine service request.

Path: what transfers → who gives/receives → receipt and usability confirmation → unresolved matters → formal completion.

Required: scope/parties/completion standard; item list with location/status/owner; access/assets/material receipt; current state; open matters and future owner; receiving confirmation. Optional: training, contacts, licenses/contracts. Exclude “what is not handed over”, secrets, and copied manuals; link to transferred sources instead.

Check: the recipient can show they received and can use every required item.
