# Handover Checklist

Use when ownership, assets, materials, or long-term responsibility move to another party; do not use for a routine service request.

Path: what transfers → who gives/receives → receipt and usability confirmation → unresolved matters → formal completion.

Required: scope/parties/completion standard (each transferred item has a stated, checkable acceptance criterion — not “receiving confirmation” alone); item list with location/status/owner; access/assets/material receipt; current state; open matters and future owner; per-item receipt sign-off by the receiving party. Optional: training, contacts, licenses/contracts. Exclude “what is not handed over”, secrets, and copied manuals; link to transferred sources instead.

Check: every required item has a location, an owner, and a checkable acceptance criterion the recipient signed against; no item rests on unqualified “confirmed” with nothing to verify.
