# Presentation

Apply this only while writing and revising the document, after the claim sheet is confirmed. Do not add facts. A causal sentence may connect only confirmed claims.

## Judgment documents

Types: `technical-research`, `architecture-design`, `feature-overview`, `issue-analysis`, `test-report`, `project-retrospective`.

The type file fixes the section order. Open with the conclusion and the confirmed constraint or evidence it rests on. Each later section opens with two prose sentences: the judgment, then why it follows from a named confirmed claim. Put tables, matrices, and steps under those sentences. Tables carry evidence; they do not replace the judgment.

Pruning test: delete a sentence only when the reader can still state what was decided and why it follows from the constraint. Keep the causal sentence. Delete chat order, rejected attempts, and author narration.

Passing check, applied internally: with the title hidden, the first screen states the conclusion, the main evidence, and what the reader should believe or do next.

## Procedure documents

Types: `user-guide`, `operations-runbook`, `deployment-plan`.

Order stays: prerequisites, actions, observable results. The reader does the task from top to bottom.

Pruning test: delete a sentence when removing it would not make the reader get stuck, misunderstand, or do the wrong thing.

## Checklists

Types: `handover-checklist`, `service-onboarding`.

A table may be the body. The opening still states, in two sentences, what complete transfer or acceptance means for the reader.
