# Presentation

Apply this only while writing and revising the document, after the claim sheet is confirmed. Do not add facts. A causal sentence may connect only confirmed claims.

## Judgment documents

Types: `technical-research`, `architecture-design`, `feature-overview`, `issue-analysis`, `test-report`, `project-retrospective`.

The type file fixes the section order. Open with a one-sentence problem statement (what question this document answers), then the conclusion and the confirmed constraint or evidence it rests on. Each later section opens with its central proposition — the reader's question, the reader's task, or the judgment, whichever the reader arrives with. The judgment and the confirmed claim it rests on must appear within the section's first screen. Put tables, matrices, and steps under the opening. Tables carry evidence; they do not replace the judgment.

Navigation sentences: a transition that only helps the reader locate themselves in the document ("the next three sections cover…") is not filler and is exempt from the pruning test. Test: if removing it makes it harder for the reader to know where they are, keep it.

Pruning test: delete a sentence only when the reader can still state what was decided and why it follows from the constraint. Keep the causal sentence. Delete chat order and author narration. Compress rejected attempts into a one-line rationale under the surviving decision (rejected X because Y, with evidence); do not recount the discussion.

Passing check, applied internally: with the title hidden, the first screen states the problem the document answers, the conclusion, the main evidence, and what the reader should believe or do next. Prefer a concrete example or scenario alongside an abstract judgment where it lowers the reader's cost of understanding.

## Procedure documents

Types: `user-guide`, `operations-runbook`, `deployment-plan`.

Order stays: prerequisites, actions, observable results. The reader does the task from top to bottom.

Pruning test: delete a sentence when removing it would not make the reader get stuck, misunderstand, or do the wrong thing.

## Checklists

Types: `handover-checklist`, `service-onboarding`.

A table may be the body. The opening still states, in two sentences, what complete transfer or acceptance means for the reader.
